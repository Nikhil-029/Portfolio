About

I'm an AI/ML engineer working on AI/ML platform development, solutions architecture, and client delivery. My work spans multi-agent systems, LLM infrastructure, data quality pipelines, and enterprise integrations — the connective tissue between "an LLM can do this" and "this runs reliably inside a business's actual workflow."

I care most about the parts of AI engineering that don't show up in a demo: confidence scoring, human-review checkpoints, duplicate detection, retry/backoff on flaky providers, and designing systems that degrade gracefully instead of hallucinating silently.
